# Scientific limitations

1. Native STD and native EDS are two observed native
   configurations, not the full NASA cFS configuration
   or mission space.
2. Native EDS qualification covers build, installation
   and evidence collection, not operational execution.
3. No EDS cFS tests or mission binaries were executed
   during this campaign. The earlier native STD pilot
   covered four test registrations, not all tests.
4. The semantic graph represents direct declarations.
   Complete physical-provider and transitive resolution
   remain outside the current qualification.
5. All license conclusions remain subject to review.
   NOASSERTION is not proof of license absence.
6. No complete vulnerability inventory or VEX analysis
   is claimed.
7. The EDS candidate list does not prove individual
   generation provenance for every file.
8. Binary identity differences do not establish
   EDS-only causal effects.
9. STATIC_MATCH is a path-and-hash observation, not
   evidence of successful execution.
10. The public verifier cannot independently hash
    original binaries, source trees or DEB archives that
    are not distributed in this package.
11. Recorded successful Lua opens do not constitute
    exhaustive runtime dependency measurement.
12. The overall research state remains GLOBAL_GREEN
    BLOCKED. The R5-B campaign is closed with limits.
